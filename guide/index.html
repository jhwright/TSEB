Adding files

  1. Choose a circle folder from the list. You can use the circle folder itself or a subfolder.
  2. Choose New → File upload, Google Docs, or Google Sheets.

Start dated file names with YYYY-MM-DD, followed by a clear title.

Keep the live version in Current and older versions in Archive. PDFs can remain PDFs.

For Word or spreadsheet conversions, keep originals until you check the content and formatting.

For filing mistakes or missing files, ask Hadas or the circle’s file keeper.

Access and sharing

Members read circle files. Designated contributors add or edit. Current Board members edit Board minutes and bylaws.

The roster keeper maintains roster PDFs and their history. Drivers use the agreed rides signup route; ask Hadas for access.

To send a link, choose Share → Copy link. A link does not grant access.

For private files, keep General access: Restricted. Ask the file keeper before sharing outside TSEB.

Private records

Confidential client records belong in the bedside coordinators’ restricted storage. Keep them out of general folders and AI tools.

Activity reports without client information can go in Music Circle with coordinator approval.

If private material appears in a general folder, do not copy or forward it. Notify Brenna, Hadas, or an administrator.
